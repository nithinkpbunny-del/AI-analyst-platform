# Playbook: campaign efficiency
1. marketing_efficiency by channel (then by campaign if asked) for the period.
2. Compare ROAS with 1 (break-even) and with the best channels; read the share of spend.
3. High spend with low ROAS is the first reallocation candidate; ROAS below 1 loses money.
4. Mention attribution limits (last-click, platform-reported revenue) before any budget move.
