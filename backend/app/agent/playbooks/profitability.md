# Playbook: profitability
1. Find revenue, discount and cost columns (describe_dataset); cost often sits in a products table joined by id.
2. run_sql: revenue, discounts, discount % of list price, cost and gross margin % by category or product.
3. Name the thinnest margins and the heaviest discounting; quantify discount leakage in rupees.
4. Separate festive-season discounting from the rest of the year when the data allows.
